Social Media Brand Audit

Nike: Social Media Marketing & Ecosystem Audit

Prepared by Jerrad Ward | Digital Marketing & Performance Analytics

1. Strategic Positioning & Core Pillars

Nike’s global social media strategy revolves around high-impact emotional storytelling, sports culture narrative, and athlete empowerment rather than direct product catalog promotion.

  • "Just Do It" Narrative: Prioritizes human resilience, cultural movements, and athletic milestones over technical sneaker specs.
  • Co-Branding & Athlete Amplification: Leverages elite global athletes such as LeBron James, Serena Williams, and Kylian Mbappé to anchor major product launches.
  • Sub-Account Ecosystem: Segregates audiences into vertical channels such as @niketraining, @nikefootball, @nikesportswear, and SNKRS.

3. Omni-Channel & DTC Integration

  • SNKRS App Funnel: Uses high-contrast social teasers to drive high-intent sneaker collectors directly into the proprietary SNKRS app, creating scarcity and viral drop moments.
  • Purpose & Sustainability Marketing: Integrates inclusive messaging, adaptive sportswear, and sustainability initiatives such as “Move to Zero” to reinforce brand affinity among younger demographics.

4. Key Strategic Recommendations

1. Native TikTok Creator Push

Shift from polished brand videos to agile, creator-led TikTok content to boost engagement with younger demographics.

2. Active Community Engagement

Transition from broadcast-style publishing to active comment section participation and community reshares.

3. Social Commerce Conversion

Implement native Instagram and TikTok Shop checkout flows to reduce purchase friction for core footwear releases.

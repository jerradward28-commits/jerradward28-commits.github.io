DTC Virtual Wine Strategy

Pour & Parcel: Social Media Marketing Blueprint

Positioning: Premium Direct-to-Consumer (DTC) virtual winery leveraging custom crush winemaking, curated pairings, and luxury doorstep packaging.

1. Target Audience Segments

Millennial & Gen Z Enthusiasts (25–40)

Focus on brand aesthetics, unboxing experiences, organic sourcing, and quick online ordering.

Corporate & Event Gifting (30–55)

Focus on high-volume holiday parcels, custom branding options, and curated sommelier pairings.

2. Core Content Pillars (Instagram & TikTok)

  • The Unboxing & Aesthetic (40%): Sensory glass pours, high-contrast packaging reels, and food pairings.
  • Behind-the-Blend (30%): Educational breakdowns of custom crush sourcing and tasting profiles without elite jargon.
  • Community & UGC (30%): Customer unboxing videos, tasting event clips, and influencer reviews.

3. Paid Media & Funnel Execution

Funnel Stage Objective Ad Format & Creative Key Metric
Top (Awareness) Reach local wine enthusiasts Meta Video Reels showcasing delivery & pour CPM (<$12)
Middle (Consideration) Lead Generation Interactive "Sommelier Pairing Guide" Quiz Cost / Lead (<$3.50)
Bottom (Conversion) First Purchase Retargeting ad with limited-batch discount code ROAS (3.5x+)
